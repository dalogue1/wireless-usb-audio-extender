Wireless USB Audio Extender 1.4.6-dev  (radio protocol v19, same as 1.4.5: the boards
still link with each other, but update both)

From the AtomS3U test outdoors at about -80 dBm (distance = far): the link was fine for
most of the hour, but in two fades of a few minutes the remote restarted itself three
times and the recording had 0.5-2 s silences.

- Remote: no more restarts for a weak signal. The "stuck transmitter" restart now needs
  the radio itself to go quiet (packets waiting, no send report for 2 s) or nothing
  acknowledged for 5 s. 1.4.5 restarted after 500 ms without an acknowledgement, which a
  fade easily causes; each restart cost about 3 s of audio and reset the noise filter.
- Remote: at most 8 audio packets (96 ms) wait in the radio. When it is fuller, the new
  packet is skipped (the local board counts it as lost: 12 ms of silence) instead of
  queueing audio that would arrive too late to play. 1.4.5 let 36 packets (430 ms) pile
  up, which also held up the link's own messages. The remote's log says once a minute
  how many it skipped.
- Drive opening: the local board waits up to 8 s (was 4 s) for the remote's temperature
  reading; without one, status.txt and the event log say how often it asked and how long
  it waited, and the remote's own temperature lines go into the event log.
