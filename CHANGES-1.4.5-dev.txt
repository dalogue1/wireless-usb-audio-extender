Wireless USB Audio Extender 1.4.5-dev  (update BOTH boards: radio protocol v19)

- Microphone audio is compressed by distance (each packet decodes on its own):
  near   = uncompressed (12 Mbit/s, about 7 % of the channel)
  normal = 8-bit mu-law, 590-byte packets (6 Mbit/s, about 10 % instead of 14 %);
           noise about 31 dB below the background in a real recording: no practical loss
  far    = IMA ADPCM, 305-byte packets (2 Mbit/s, about 16 % instead of 45 %); noise
           about 14-19 dB below the background in the 3-10 kHz band (~0.1-0.2 dB on
           faint calls). Same reach as before.
- Local board: the microphone buffer is 150 ms (was 68 ms), so radio hold-ups up to about
  130 ms no longer empty it (they caused the PC-side underruns/gaps). Speaker mode's
  buffer on the remote is unchanged.
- Drive opening: the remote's temperature reading no longer gives up when its send-queue
  count is stuck at 1; it waits up to 0.4 s, then reads anyway.
