Wireless USB Audio Extender 1.4.4-dev  (update BOTH boards: radio protocol v17)

Chip temperature is no longer read while audio streams. Each board reads it:
  - at its start, before its radio starts (no radio pause; in events.txt), and
  - when the EXTENDER drive opens (the audio stops then anyway; in status.txt).
The 10-minute reads are gone: each needed the radio stopped, cost a short audio gap,
and 1.4.3 still saw the AtomS3U remote restart at its first read with an empty queue.
The `temperature` setting is removed; an old line in config.txt is ignored.
Hourly/event health lines no longer show temperatures. 75 C warnings remain (start and
drive-opening readings).
