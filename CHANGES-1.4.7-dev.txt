Wireless USB Audio Extender 1.4.7-dev  (radio protocol v20: update both boards)

- New config.txt setting: audio_compression = auto | none | low | high (both boards).
  It chooses the microphone audio format independently of the radio rate:
    none = uncompressed 16-bit (1166-byte packets), low = 8-bit mu-law (590 bytes),
    high = IMA ADPCM (305 bytes). auto (the default) keeps 1.4.5's choice by distance:
    near = none, normal = low, far = high. High means most compression (smallest
    packets, most loss), not best sound.
- status.txt, the log and the settings lines name the format in use, and the setting
  when it isn't auto, e.g. "distance far (2 Mbit/s, mu-law audio, audio_compression low)".
  At near they now also say "uncompressed audio".
- Docs: distance.md has a new "Audio compression" section; configuration.md,
  building.md, speaker-mode.md and the example config.txt / status.txt updated.

Also in this build (from 1.4.6-dev):
- The remote no longer restarts itself for a weak signal: the "stuck transmitter"
  restart needs the radio silent for 2 s or nothing acknowledged for 5 s.
- At most 8 audio packets (96 ms) wait in the remote's radio; newer audio is skipped
  (counted as lost) rather than queueing audio that would arrive too late.
- Drive opening waits up to 8 s for the remote's temperature and logs why if none came.
